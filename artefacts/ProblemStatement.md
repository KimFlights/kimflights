Planning trips is meant to be an exciting time, but often they are plagued with the stress of finding accommodations, deciding on food, locating comfortable and affordable housing, searching for activities, and namely booking flights. 
After deciding on a flight, it can be hard to determine if that is the cheapest flight. In addition to basic functionalities like booking flights, cancelling flights, rescheduling flights (aligning with company policies), receiving updates, and a booking reference/QR for a ticket, users will be able to see flight catalogues in a whole new way. Flights that are cheaper than normal will be highlighted, and cheap connecting flights for secondary destinations will also be highlighted when booking a primary destination. 
Customers can search flights and sort/filter by date/time, price range, connecting flights, and airline. After they have found a flight, they may save the flight or may choose to book. Then, they can choose to pay by account balance/credit cards. After a successful booking, customers will receive the booking reference, boarding times, any connecting flights, departure time, and QR code.
Additionally, anyone will be able to access the flight statistics which include pricing,  flight time, any delay, their booking reference destinations, and total flights made for each destination.
